One line per milestone: date and time, what was decided or built, and with which tool (Codex, Nemotron, Cala).

- 14/11: project reorganized for two machines: backend/ + app/ (Expo), docs/API.md contract, coordination with `equipo` (claude-central).
- 14/11: Cala query canonizer (issue #9), built with Codex: 4 variants of one question (with and without accents, English, Catalan) return the same answer with 3 calls. Finding: Amenitiz's Series B appears as EUR 3,000 M (the article's booking figure) and has no linked investors.

- 14/11: canonizer tested with 7 question types (founders, acquisitions, total funding, investors, executives, and fintech and climate tech lists): 21 phrasings in 4 languages → 7 queries, identical answers within each group. Fixed the numerical_observations format (Cala requires a dictionary).
- 14/11: canonizer benchmark (9 questions × 3 phrasings): direct, 0/9 consistent groups and 27 calls; canonized, 9/9 and 9 calls on a cold cache. Error report in docs/CALA-CONSISTENCY-REPORT.md and figures in docs/BENCHMARK-CANONIZER.md.
- 14/11: benchmark with a third mode (natural language directly to knowledge/query): also 0/9 consistent groups; figures, companies, and field names changed. Canonizer: 9/9.
- 14/11: benchmark with an agent connected to Cala via MCP (Codex): 1/9 questions consistent; always uses knowledge_search, rewrites the question (24 different texts in 25 calls), and did not query Cala in 2 of 27 cases.
- 14/11: benchmark with an agent via MCP with Cala's guide loaded: uses structured queries and entities, but 0/9 questions consistent; with the guide, it does not make things up when MCP fails (8 of 33 connections failed).
- 14/11: shift in focus to 'Cala at the center' (router, Cala tables, claim verification, journal); plan in docs/internal/PLAN-CALA-CENTRO.md, contract updated, README and documentation rewritten, Codex log in docs/CODEX.md.
- 14/11: roles set: Claude orchestrates and designs (does not write code), Codex programs, Gemini is the AI in the product. Gemini replaces NVIDIA; models selected by measuring the router task (gemini-3.5-flash-lite: ~1 s, 3/3). Shared Aiven Valkey cache merged (#42).
- 14/11: open questions without a company go to knowledge_search with a canonical question and sources (#49). New task allocation to balance sessions: backend, app, and canonizer go to fabricio; bau coordinates; each machine merges its PRs with tests passing. Aiven Valkey cache active: 222 entries uploaded.
- 14/11 (evening): shift in focus to **Cala Match**, a two-sided marketplace (Raising / Investing) with an affinity feed, detail view, chat, and context; the entire app is in English. Adrian joins (four machines). Stitch demo design in `docs/design/stitch/`. Measured: loading a 185-node subgraph takes ~14–18 s of actual Cala work, and local BFS takes 0.13 ms.
- 14/11 (evening): Cala client optimizer (#63). Pacing by endpoint type (8 s for `knowledge/*`, 0.7 s for entities), concurrency of 2 for entities, `Retry-After` after a 429, and a single call for identical simultaneous requests. Measured against live Cala, without cache, with searches for distinct entities: 30 calls at 0.7 s and concurrency 2 in 22.6 s (1.33/s), 40 at 0.5 s and concurrency 2 in 21.2 s (1.89/s), 40 at 0.3 s and concurrency 3 in 33.7 s (1.19/s); **no 429s in the three runs**. More concurrency does not speed things up: Cala responds more slowly. Previously, with a fixed 8 s delay, 19 calls took 162 s.
- 14/11 (evening): shared rate limit in Valkey (#85): per-window counter (`ratelimit:v1:cala:<clase>:<ventana>`, INCR+EXPIRE) per endpoint class, in addition to local pacing; if Valkey fails, the local 30 s limit continues. Verified with tests using fake Valkey; measured against live Cala with two processes at once (`medir_limite.py --sin-cache-local`, entities only, 20 calls each, 0.7 s interval, window equal to the interval with quota 1): 40 calls in 27.9 s (1.43 combined calls/s; processes took 18.6 s and 27.9 s), **0 429 errors**, 37 shared waits, and 1 limiter error (Valkey; continued with the local limit). `--sin-cache` also disables the limiter; `--sin-cache-local` disables only the response cache.
- 15/11: final product shape (sign-up with Raiser or Investor role, Explore and My events, floating assistant, profile); the new website is being built with Gemini. Backend: Cala fallback when something is not in the graph (#98), and presentation and PDF drafts in `docs/presentation/`.
